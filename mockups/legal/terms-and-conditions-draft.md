## 3. How the opportunity auction works

Flipsta's AI sources time-limited resale opportunities and presents them to Resellers with key identifying details withheld until a binding action is taken ("blind teaser" mechanism), to protect the commercial value of the opportunity. Each opportunity carries an action clock, the length of which varies by the opportunity's urgency tier, shown at the time of listing. A Reseller may bid, or pay the displayed instant-win price to win the opportunity immediately. Placing a bid or paying an instant-win price is a binding commitment to pay; payment is captured (not merely authorised) at that point. When the action clock expires, the highest live bid wins the opportunity. ## 4. Payments and escrow

All payments for opportunities and marketplace purchases are processed via Flipsta's payment provider (Stripe) using Stripe Connect. Funds paid by a Buyer for a marketplace purchase are held by Flipsta and released to the selling Reseller only after delivery is confirmed, or after a Reseller-selected hold period of up to two weeks has elapsed without a return being raised, whichever is later. Flipsta does not hold client money outside of this payment processing arrangement.

## 5. Marketplace listings and order matching

Where multiple Resellers list the same product and condition, listings are pooled into a single marketplace entry. Orders are matched to the Reseller offering the lowest price first; where two or more Resellers are tied on price, the listing made first takes priority. A Reseller may instead set a limit-sell price and have their stock held until a matching order is found or the listing is cancelled.

## 6. Commission and fees

Flipsta charges a commission on marketplace sales, calculated as a percentage of sale price that varies according to the Reseller's subscription tier or, for Trade Sellers, their monthly sales volume, as set out in the current fee schedule available on the Platform. Subscription fees, where applicable, are billed [monthly/annually — confirm] and are non-refundable except as required by law.

## 7. The buyback guarantee

Where a Reseller has purchased buyback guarantee cover on a specific opportunity, Flipsta will pay the guaranteed amount if the item genuinely fails to sell, subject to the Reseller: (a) actively listing the item for sale at or below the platform's original estimated resale price for a minimum of 14 days with evidence of the listing, and (b) if still unsold, offering the item at cost — either on the open market or back to Flipsta's own liquidation pool — for a further 48–72 hours before a claim is paid. Claims that do not meet both conditions will not be honoured. Flipsta reserves the right to investigate any claim for signs of abuse of this guarantee.

## 8. Buyer Want requests

A Buyer may post a Buyer Want specifying an item and the maximum price they are willing to pay. This is not a binding offer to any specific Reseller until a Reseller's responding offer is accepted through the Platform's matching process; once matched, the transaction is binding on both parties in the same way as any other Platform sale.
